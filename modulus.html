HOW TO PLAY

  • Both champions sit on a ring of N positions — the integers mod N.
  • Each round you both secretly pick a residue m ∈ {0 … N−1}, then reveal at once.
  • Clash: let d = (your m − their m) mod N. If d is in the lower half (1…(N−1)/2) you win and deal d damage. Upper half, you lose. Equal residues parry.
  • Move: your same number is how many steps you sweep clockwise. Any shard you pass or land on is collected.
  • The number that wins a clash is rarely the number that grabs the shard you want — that squeeze is the game.
  • Win: drain the opponent's HP to zero, or collect 5 shards first.
  • No dice, no hidden stats. Every residue beats exactly half the others, so nothing is overpowered — you win by reading your opponent.
